NAME
BoobieTrackr

SUBTITLE
Breast, bottle & pumping log

PROMOTIONAL TEXT
One less thing to remember. Log feeds, switch sides, and share your baby's journal with your partner—from your iPhone or Apple Watch.

DESCRIPTION
Was it left or right last time?

BoobieTrackr keeps your baby's feeds in one simple timeline, so you can spend less time remembering and more time with your baby.

LOG EVERY FEED
Track breastfeeding, bottles, and pumping. Choose a side, add milk amounts when you know them, and adjust the time with a tap. Changes save automatically.

A TIMER WHEN YOU NEED ONE
Start a breastfeeding timer and follow it from your iPhone's Lock Screen. Tap Done to save the duration, or switch sides to finish one feed and start the next. Prefer a quick entry? Leave the timer off.

MADE FOR NIGHT FEEDS
A dark interface, soft pink accents, and large controls keep the essentials easy to find, even in the small hours.

SHARE THE JOURNAL
Invite your partner to the same baby's journal through iCloud. Both of you can add and edit feeds. Keep separate journals for multiple babies and switch between them whenever you need to.

QUICK ENTRIES ON YOUR WRIST
Start a left or right feed, log a bottle, or record pumping from Apple Watch. Save a breastfeeding timer or switch sides right in the Watch app.

YOUR HISTORY, AT A GLANCE
Browse the daily timeline, jump to a date, and export a baby's feeding history as a CSV file.

No ads. No separate account to create. iCloud sync and partner invitations use your Apple Account.

REVIEW NOTES
The app is a feeding journal for iPhone with a companion Apple Watch app. It does not require an app-specific login. An Apple Account signed into iCloud is required for cloud sync and invitations. Feeding records can be entered locally without a separate account. Breastfeeding timers are optional; bottle and pumping entries support optional milliliter amounts. Partner invitations use Apple's native CloudKit sharing interface. The app does not provide medical advice or use HealthKit.
